## Chapter 3: The water cycle

- **Evaporation**: the sun heats water into vapour
- **Condensation**: vapour cools into clouds
- **Precipitation**: rain, snow, hail
- **Collection**: rivers, lakes, oceans, ground water

> [!note] Remember
> The cycle has no start and no end.

Water moves between the oceans, the air and the land, powered by the sun and pulled back down by
gravity.
